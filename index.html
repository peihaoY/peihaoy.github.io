About Me

I am a Ph.D. candidate in Computer Science and Engineering at Michigan State University, advised by Huacheng Zeng. My research focuses on resource allocation, spectrum efficiency, scheduling, AI-driven optimization, 5G open-radio access networks (O-RAN), and integrated sensing and communication (ISAC).

During my master's studies, I was advised by Xuerong Cui.
