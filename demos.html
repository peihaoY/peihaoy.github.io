5G/6G and Open RAN

O-RAN · DRL · xApp

Near-Real-Time O-RAN Control

An end-to-end experiment connecting two distributed units (DU1 and DU2) with a near-real-time RIC/xApp. Terminal telemetry and the accompanying performance trace provide a compact view of online resource-control behavior.

Digital Twin · CIR/CFR · Replay

AODT Channel Replay

A digital-twin playback aligns the mapped environment with raw channel impulse response (CIR) and channel frequency response (CFR) views. The synchronized panels expose delay-domain structure and frequency-domain channel variation during replay.

Wireless Sensing and ISAC

Outdoor Test · CSI · Trajectory

Outdoor CSI Field Measurement

An outdoor measurement combines the field-test setup, mobile trajectory and altitude context, and synchronized CSI replay across four receive ports. The side-by-side views relate movement context to channel magnitude and unwrapped phase.

ISAC · Range–Angle · Doppler

50 m Azimuth Sensing View

An outdoor sensing view pairs the physical scene with real-time azimuth–range magnitude and azimuth–Doppler velocity visualizations. The display uses a 50 m range scale to present directional sensing results as the scene evolves.

Experimental Systems

VR · E2E Latency · Mobility

VR Device Latency and Trajectory

A synchronized demonstration of the VR user, device view, end-to-end latency readout, and indoor route map. Together, the panels show how the system is observed while the user moves through the building.

Testbed · Network Platform · Deployment

Wireless Network Experimental Platform

The experimental-platform view connects an indoor route map with the rack-mounted radio and network equipment used by the testbed, providing physical deployment context for end-to-end measurements.
