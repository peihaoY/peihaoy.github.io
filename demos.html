Research Projects

Wireless Networking · Sensing · Experimental Systems

Research Demonstrations

Selected projects in AI-driven wireless networks and sensing. These demonstrations span OFDM-based ISAC, real-time 5G channel measurement, O-RAN digital twins and near-real-time control, private-network experimentation, and jamming-resilient mmWave communication.

Wireless Sensing and ISAC

5G ISAC · Range–Azimuth · Doppler

Monostatic 5G ISAC Platform and AoA Estimation

This prototype reuses OFDM communication signals for sensing, extracting range, angle of arrival, and Doppler information from the received echoes. The video pairs the outdoor scene with real-time range–azimuth and azimuth–Doppler views to show target location and motion over a 50 m sensing range.

5G CSI · SRS · Four RX Ports

Measurement of Real-Time Uplink CSI in MSU's Private 5G Network

Uplink sounding reference signals allow a commercial 5G radio unit to estimate fine-grained channel state information, including amplitude, phase, frequency response, and timing advance. The video visualizes the CSI measured across four receive ports as a smartphone user alternates between standing and walking.

Digital Twin for 5G O-RAN

Field Measurement · CSI · Mobility

Outdoor CSI Measurement for Digital-Twin Evaluation

This field campaign records uplink CSI along a known outdoor route to provide measured channel data for wireless digital-twin evaluation. The synchronized views align user position and altitude with channel magnitude and unwrapped phase across four receive ports, revealing how the channel evolves with mobility.

O-RAN Digital Twin · CIR/CFR · Replay

AODT Channel and Environment Replay

This digital-twin replay synchronizes a mapped environment with channel impulse response (CIR) and channel frequency response (CFR) measurements. The coordinated views make it possible to inspect how movement and geometry appear in the delay and frequency domains, supporting channel-model validation and digital-twin analysis.

5G O-RAN Resource Allocation and Mobility Management

5G Edge AI · VR · Mobility

5G Edge AI Offloading for a VR Headset

This demonstration uses 5G edge AI offloading to support a computation-intensive VR application: the headset streams camera frames to an edge server and receives inference results in real time. The synchronized device view, indoor route, and round-trip latency trace show how application performance changes as the user moves through the building.

O-RAN · DRL · Near-RT RIC

Near-Real-Time O-RAN Resource Slicing

A deep-reinforcement-learning xApp runs in the near-real-time RIC and uses key performance measurements delivered over the E2 interface—including throughput, latency, and block error rate—to adapt resource slicing under changing channels and QoS demands. The video connects the two distributed units, xApp telemetry, and performance traces to expose the live control loop.

Experimental Systems

Private 5G · O-RAN · Testbed

MSU Private 5G O-RAN Experimental Platform

MSU's private 5G network combines commercial O-RUs and an O-CUDU with smartphones and compute resources for real-world wireless experimentation. The video relates the indoor deployment map to the rack-mounted radio, network, and edge-computing equipment used to evaluate xApps, rApps, network optimization, and end-to-end system performance.

Anti-Jamming 5G mmWave Communications

Anti-Jamming · 28 GHz · Beamforming

Jamming-Resilient 5G mmWave Receiver

This project combines Bayesian-optimization-based analog beamforming with modified-MMSE digital detection to suppress interference in both the analog and digital domains. The GNU Radio/USRP demonstration shows the receiver workflow and packet recovery on a 28 GHz over-the-air testbed under strong jamming.
