Wireless Sensing and ISAC

ISAC · Range–Angle · Doppler

Monostatic 5G ISAC Platform and AOA Estimation

An outdoor sensing view pairs the physical scene with real-time azimuth–range magnitude and azimuth–Doppler velocity visualizations. The display uses a 50 m range scale to present directional sensing results as the scene evolves.

CSI · Four RX Ports · Measurement

Measurement of Real-Time Uplink CSI in MSU's Private 5G Network

5G uplink Channel State Information (CSI) includes not only MIMO channel-matrix coefficients but also Timing Advance (TA). Because 5G operates in a master–slave mode, the network can proactively hand over a smartphone from one gNB to another. This video shows real-time CSI measurements at a commercial base station from a smartphone while the user is standing and walking.

Digital Twin for 5G O-RAN

Outdoor Test · CSI · Trajectory

Outdoor CSI Field Measurement

An outdoor measurement combines the field-test setup, mobile trajectory and altitude context, and synchronized CSI replay across four receive ports. The side-by-side views relate movement context to channel magnitude and unwrapped phase.

Digital Twin · CIR/CFR · Replay

AODT Channel Replay

A digital-twin playback aligns the mapped environment with raw channel impulse response (CIR) and channel frequency response (CFR) views. The synchronized panels expose delay-domain structure and frequency-domain channel variation during replay.

Anti-Jamming 5G mmWave Communications

Anti-Jamming · 5G mmWave · USRP

GNU Radio / USRP Receiver Workflow

This project develops a joint analog–digital beamforming receiver for reliable 5G mmWave communication under strong jamming. Online-learning Bayesian optimization adapts the analog beam, while modified MMSE digital detection suppresses residual interference. A 28 GHz over-the-air prototype demonstrates robust packet decoding across diverse targeted-jamming scenarios.
